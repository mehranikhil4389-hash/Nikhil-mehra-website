Experience the Real Kangra

Kangra Roots Trails is a community-based tourism venture connecting travellers with lesser-known destinations, villages, local hosts, artisans, farmers and guides.

Our digital-first model makes local experiences easier to discover and book, while helping communities participate directly in tourism.
